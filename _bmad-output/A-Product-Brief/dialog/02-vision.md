## Opening

Materials already existed (SPEC.md's Why), so Saga opened by quoting it and asking whether it still held, plus follow-ups on what specifically breaks for a Colombian collector today, the longer-term ambition, and a picture of the product working well.

## Key Exchanges

**On what breaks today:** No local sellers to discover. Two paths exist: eBay (international shipping + import taxes) or informal WhatsApp/Telegram seller communities — no formal local marketplace.

**On long-term ambition:** "The go to pokemon tcg platform in colombia" — explicitly Colombia-scoped, not immediately multi-country or multi-TCG.

**On what "working well" looks like — concrete current-workflow example:**
> "as a colombian collector if I want a card, I search it up on ebay, then, to see wether the price is right I look the price on pricecharting, and once I purchase it I log the card into collectr."

The vision is collapsing that three-tool chain into one platform, plus organizing collections into virtual binders and enabling direct contact with Colombian sellers without the shipping/import-tax hit.

## Reflection Checkpoint

**Synthesis presented:** Become the go-to Pokémon TCG platform in Colombia, replacing the eBay + PriceCharting + Collectr chain and the shipping/import-tax cost of buying abroad, with one place to search, check market trends, buy locally, organize collections into virtual binders, and track wishlist cards.

**User response:** Confirmed ("yes") — no corrections needed.

## Final Vision Statement

> The go-to Pokémon TCG platform in Colombia: one place where a Colombian collector can search the catalog, check market trends, buy directly from local sellers, organize their collection into virtual binders, and track wishlist cards — replacing the current fragmented workflow of eBay (buying) + PriceCharting (pricing) + Collectr (collection logging), plus the international shipping and import-tax costs that come with sourcing from abroad.

## Key Insights Captured

- Three-tool chain (eBay → PriceCharting → Collectr) is the concrete status quo pain, not hypothetical.
- No formal local marketplace exists today — informal WhatsApp/Telegram communities are the closest substitute (consistent with SPEC.md's individual-seller external-contact model).
- Differentiator vs. existing US-oriented tools: Colombia-first, avoids cross-border shipping/import-tax friction.
- Scope is deliberately Colombia-specific for now — not immediately multi-country or multi-TCG.

# Casual Buyer — Priority 3

## Who They Are

Browsing more than collecting — comparing listings, checking market context, wanting a low-friction way to reach a seller without commitment. Often the same person as the Individual Seller on a different day; not a disjoint population, per the Product Brief's "graduate" framing.

## Psychological Profile

**Non-committal and comparison-oriented.** Doesn't want to create an account or fill out a profile just to ask "is this still available?" Trusts price sanity-checking as the primary filter before ever messaging a seller — if a listing reads as obviously overpriced, they move on without engaging.

Less identity-invested in the hobby-expert self-image than the Release-Driven Collector; this persona's relationship to the platform is lighter-weight and more exploratory.

## Internal State

**Mildly skeptical** of any asking price until independently checked — the default assumption is "verify, don't trust," carried over from the current status quo of foreign price trackers being the only reference. Overall mood is **relaxed and low-stakes**, not urgent.

## Usage Context

**1. Access/Discovery:** Arrives via browse/search, not necessarily tied to a release-date event.

**2. Emotional State:** Relaxed, exploratory, low commitment.

**3. Behavior Pattern:** Compares a listing's asking price against a reference value before deciding it's worth a message; skips listings that read as overpriced.

**4. Decision Criteria:** A fast, trustworthy price-fairness read; low-friction seller contact with no heavy signup step.

**5. Success Outcome:** Confidently tells fair pricing from inflated pricing and reaches out to a seller — or completes an in-platform purchase from a verified business — without friction.

## Relationship to Business Goals

- ✅ **Goal 1 (go-to platform):** The Success Criteria's "never leave the platform to sanity-check a price" bar is written directly against this persona's behavior.
- ✅ **Goal 2 (business revenue):** When buying from a Verified Business, this persona is the one converting into in-platform, commission-generating purchases.

# Release-Driven Collector — Priority 1 (Primary Archetype)

## Who They Are

Follows new Pokémon TCG set releases closely and continuously picks up individual cards of interest, logging purchases roughly monthly. Today's routine is three disconnected tools stitched together by hand: a price-guide site for set awareness → eBay to buy → PriceCharting to verify the price wasn't inflated → Collectr to log the purchase into their collection record.

## Psychological Profile

Sees themselves as an **informed, serious collector**, not a casual browser — the routine of cross-checking price before buying is a point of pride and identity, not just diligence. This makes fragmentation across three tools feel like **friction against their own competence**, not just inconvenience: when the tools disagree slightly, it undermines their confidence in their own collection data.

They are **release-driven**, meaning their engagement has a rhythm tied to external events (set drops), not a constant background browse. This matters for design: the moments right after a new set releases are high-intent, high-frequency windows where trust signals matter most.

## Internal State

Feels **scattered and slightly distrustful** when managing purchases across three disconnected tools — each new purchase is a small reconciliation chore, and there's a nagging uncertainty about whether the numbers actually line up. Feels **in control and satisfied** when catalog, binder, and price all point back to the same record without extra effort.

## Usage Context

**1. Access/Discovery:** Checks the platform proactively around new set release dates; also browses casually week to week outside those windows.

**2. Emotional State:** Focused and deliberate when evaluating a purchase; a low background anxiety about paying above the real market price.

**3. Behavior Pattern:** Cross-references price before buying, checks their binder/wishlist to avoid buying a duplicate, updates the binder immediately after a purchase.

**4. Decision Criteria:** Price trustworthiness (does this look like a real Colombian market price, not inflated?), seller credibility, whether the card slots cleanly into an existing binder page or wishlist.

**5. Success Outcome:** Buys with confidence the price was fair, and the binder reflects the purchase without manual re-entry.

## Relationship to Business Goals

- ✅ **Goal 1 (go-to platform):** Highest-frequency engagement of any persona; the one most likely to make the platform their default over Collectr.
- ✅ **Goal 3 (ship non-negotiable core by Nov 2026):** The only persona using tracker + binder + wishlist end-to-end before marketplace liquidity exists — the direct proof point of "useful from day one on the collection side."

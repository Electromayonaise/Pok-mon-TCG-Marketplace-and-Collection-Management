# Verified Business / Specialized Store — Priority 4

## Who They Are

A solo seller-as-business (already selling informally via Instagram/social) or a small team/shop, applying for verification to gain a real distribution channel and credibility beyond informal social selling. Represents the business-side revenue track — deliberately monetized later than the collection-tracking core.

## Psychological Profile

**Professional and credibility-seeking**, thinking in terms of visibility ROI and predictable economics rather than hobbyist trading. Distinguishes themselves consciously from an anonymous social-media seller — verification is worth applying for specifically because it converts "just another Instagram account" into a signal buyers can trust.

**Wary of upfront-cost risk** before the channel proves itself — this directly informed the platform's own prepaid-balance, no-invoicing design decision (Platform Mechanics), which suggests this persona's anxiety about unpredictable costs is treated as a first-class design constraint already, not just a nice-to-have.

## Internal State

Feels **motivated by the legitimacy/credibility signal** verification confers. Feels **anxious** about two specific things: commission economics being unpredictable, and being mistaken for an unverified, anonymous seller despite having gone through the process.

## Usage Context

**1. Access/Discovery:** Applies for verification with legal-identity/external-presence evidence; once approved, manages listings and messaging day-to-day.

**2. Emotional State:** Professional, outcome-focused, with background anxiety around application-review turnaround and commission-balance management.

**3. Behavior Pattern:** Tops up the commission balance proactively to avoid a listing pause; monitors in-app messages and purchase confirmations.

**4. Decision Criteria:** Predictable commission economics (no surprise invoicing), a credibility signal strong enough to justify the time spent applying.

**5. Success Outcome:** Gets approved, becomes visible, and converts buyers in-platform with commission behaving predictably.

## Relationship to Business Goals

- ✅ **Goal 2 (business revenue):** This persona *is* the business-side revenue/viability track directly — commission on in-platform sales is the primary revenue stream.
- ✅ **Goal 1 (go-to platform):** Verified-business inventory is what eventually gives the platform credibility as "the place to buy," beyond individual listings alone.

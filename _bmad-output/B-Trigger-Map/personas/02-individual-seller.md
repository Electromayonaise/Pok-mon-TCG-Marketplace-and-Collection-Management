# Individual Seller — Priority 2

## Who They Are

A collector who occasionally sells or trades — trading up, thinning a collection, or flipping a pulled card — without wanting a formal storefront or business paperwork. Often the same person as the Casual Buyer or the Release-Driven Collector on a different day, not a distinct population. Today's channel is informal WhatsApp/Telegram groups with no search and no structure.

## Psychological Profile

Values **speed and low friction** above formality — deliberately doesn't want the overhead of "running a shop," and self-identifies as a hobbyist, not a merchant. This is a real boundary, not laziness: the Verified Business path exists precisely for people who *do* want that overhead, and this persona explicitly isn't them.

Carries genuine **wariness from the informal-market status quo** — WhatsApp/Telegram groups have no way to signal seriousness or verify a buyer will actually follow through, so trust has to be rebuilt from scratch in every interaction.

## Internal State

Feels **cautious and slightly exposed** when publishing contact information or agreeing to a deal with a stranger — the fear isn't abstract, it's the concrete pattern of being ghosted or having a buyer no-show after negotiation. Feels **relieved** when a match is local, straightforward, and completes without drama.

## Usage Context

**1. Access/Discovery:** Lists a card/bundle after a lightweight one-time profile step; browses other sellers' listings to gauge asking prices.

**2. Emotional State:** Guarded when publishing contact info; wary about buyer follow-through once a deal is negotiated off-platform.

**3. Behavior Pattern:** Marks a listing as trade-open or sale-only; watches for trade offers or contact requests; negotiates on-platform, arranges the actual handoff off-platform.

**4. Decision Criteria:** Buyer proximity (in-person pickup avoids shipping), signals that a buyer is serious (has a real profile, not anonymous).

**5. Success Outcome:** Gets a serious local contact or trade offer without wading through unstructured group-chat noise.

## Relationship to Business Goals

- ✅ **Goal 3 (non-negotiable core):** The trading system and individual-seller flow are explicitly fixed, non-negotiable scope — this persona is the direct beneficiary.
- ✅ **Goal 1 (go-to platform):** Contributes the seller-side liquidity the "go-to platform" bar depends on over time.

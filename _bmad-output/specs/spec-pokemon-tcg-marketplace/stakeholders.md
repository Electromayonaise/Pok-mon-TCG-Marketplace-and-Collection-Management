# Stakeholders

Spec-authored companion to `SPEC.md`. Source: `docs/Problem-statement.pdf`, Stakeholders section. Each group's goals are load-bearing for which capability matters to whom, but don't fit the five-field kernel as a flat list — kept here as a per-actor matrix.

| Stakeholder | Goals |
|---|---|
| Collectors and hobbyists | Reliable information about cards, prices, availability, and sellers; tools to organize/track collections and plan future acquisitions. |
| Buyers and casual users | Discover products, compare listings, understand market context, communicate with sellers through a convenient process. |
| Individual sellers | A simple way to publish cards, bundles, and products; expose listings to interested users; establish direct communication with buyers without necessarily processing transactions through the platform. |
| Pokémon TCG businesses and specialized stores | Greater visibility, a professional profile, a mechanism for publishing inventory, and credibility through verified business information and user reviews. |
| Platform administrators | Review/approve business applications, moderate listings and user-generated information, manage platform policies, maintain marketplace/catalog reliability and integrity. |
| The Pokémon TCG community | A centralized, structured source of information about cards, sets, prices, products, and collecting trends — valuable even without an associated transaction. |

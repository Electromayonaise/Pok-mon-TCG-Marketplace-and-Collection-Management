## What Belongs Here

The Design System captures reusable patterns that emerge during UX Design (Phase 4). It is not designed upfront — it crystallizes from real page specifications.

**What goes here:** Design Tokens, Components, Patterns, Visual Design explorations, Assets.
**What does NOT go here:** Page-specific content (`C-UX-Scenarios/`), business logic/API specs, aspirational components nobody uses yet.

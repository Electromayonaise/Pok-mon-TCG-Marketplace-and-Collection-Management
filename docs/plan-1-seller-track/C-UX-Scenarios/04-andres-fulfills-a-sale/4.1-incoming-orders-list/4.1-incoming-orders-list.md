## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Show Andrés every incoming Order against his listings (FR-S7).

**User Situation:** A notification tells him he has a new sale. He checks his phone between customers at the shop — a quick glance, not a sit-down session.

**Success Criteria:** He can see every order and its current tri-state status at a glance, and jump into any one that needs his attention.

**Entry Points:**
- Notification tap (new order).
- Account menu → "Incoming orders."

**Exit Points:**
- → 4.2 Order Detail / Confirm Payment (tap a row).

## Page States

| State | When | Appearance | Actions |
|-------|------|------------|---------|
| Loading | Page opens | Skeleton rows | — |
| Populated | Orders exist | Full list, newest first | Tap a row |
| Empty | No orders yet | Empty-state: "No orders yet — sales against your listings will show up here" | Back to account menu |
| Error | Fetch fails | Inline error: "Couldn't load your orders — check your connection and try again" | Retry |

---

## Technical Notes

- This page never shows a "Closed" order as removed from the list — closed orders remain visible (read-only history), consistent with the Buyer track's own order-history retention pattern.
- Andrés's read access to these Order records is governed by the shared cross-track Order read-ownership decision made in the Buyer/Collector track's ARCHITECTURE.md (AD-15) — this page assumes that access is already granted, it does not itself decide the ACL.

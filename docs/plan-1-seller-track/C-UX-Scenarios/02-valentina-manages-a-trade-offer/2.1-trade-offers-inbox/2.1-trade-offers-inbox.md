## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Show incoming trade offers on Valentina's listings (FR-S3).

**User Situation:** A push/in-app notification tells her someone offered a trade. She opens the inbox to see what's waiting.

**Mental State:** Curiosity, mild caution — a trade means giving something up, not just receiving money, so she wants to see the offer before committing to anything.

**Success Criteria:** She can see every open offer at a glance, distinguishing new/unseen from already-viewed.

**Entry Points:**
- Notification tap.
- Account menu → "Trade offers."

**Exit Points:**
- → 2.2 Trade Offer Detail (tap a card).

## Page States

| State | When | Appearance | Actions |
|-------|------|------------|---------|
| Loading | Page opens | Skeleton cards | — |
| Populated | Offers exist | Full list, newest first | Tap a card |
| Empty | No trade offers exist yet | Empty-state illustration + "No trade offers yet — offers on your listings will show up here" | Back to My Sales |
| Error | Fetch fails | Inline error: "Couldn't load your trade offers — check your connection and try again" | Retry |

---

## Technical Notes

- Withdrawn and Declined offers remain visible in this list (not deleted) — per EXPERIENCE.md's trade-offer lifecycle, all 5 states are terminal-or-not but none are hidden, since Valentina may want to review a past offer's history.

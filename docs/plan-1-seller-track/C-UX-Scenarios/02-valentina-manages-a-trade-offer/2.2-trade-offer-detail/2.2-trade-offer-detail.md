## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let Valentina review one trade offer's full detail and Accept or Decline it (FR-S3).

**Success Criteria:** She can see exactly what's being offered against exactly what of hers, and record a clear decision.

**Entry Points:**
- 2.1, tapping a trade-offer-card.

**Exit Points:**
- Accept → 2.3 Trade Completion Confirmation (once both sides eventually confirm — see Page States).
- Decline → back to 2.1, card now shows Declined.

## Page States

| State | When | Appearance | Actions |
|-------|------|------------|---------|
| Offered | Fresh offer, no decision yet | Accept/Decline both enabled | Accept, Decline |
| Confirming accept | Accept tapped, confirmation prompt open | `trade-offer-accept-confirm-prompt` shown, both original buttons disabled | Confirm, Cancel |
| Accepted | Valentina accepted | Badge shows "Accepted — waiting for both sides to confirm completion"; both action buttons removed | Navigate to 2.3 when ready to confirm completion |
| Declined | Valentina declined | Badge shows "Declined"; no actions remain | Back to 2.1 |
| Withdrawn | Other party withdrew before Valentina acted | Badge shows "Withdrawn by the other side"; no actions remain | Back to 2.1 |
| Completed | Both sides confirmed (via 2.3) | Badge shows "Completed" | Back to 2.1 |

---

## Technical Notes

- Accept does not immediately mark the trade Completed — per CAP-25/26, completion requires mutual confirmation from both sides (see 2.3). This page only records Valentina's Accept/Decline decision.
- A trade cannot be re-Declined once Accepted, nor re-Accepted once Declined — the state transition is one-directional per offer.
- Accept-vs-withdraw race: the other party can withdraw an offer at any point before Valentina's Accept is recorded, including the instant between her tapping Confirm and the request completing. The server rejects an Accept against an offer that is no longer `Offered`, and the client treats that rejection the same as a fresh page-load into the Withdrawn state — never as a generic error.

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Let Valentina confirm the physical/practical exchange happened, as her half of the mutual-completion requirement (FR-S4, CAP-25/26).

**Success Criteria:** Her confirmation is recorded; the trade shows Completed only once the other side has also confirmed.

**Entry Points:**
- 2.2, once an offer is Accepted.
- 2.1, tapping a Completed trade-offer-card to review a trade she already confirmed.

**Exit Points:**
- Back to 2.1/2.2 once confirmed (or once both sides have confirmed and the trade is Completed).

## Page States

| State | When | Appearance | Actions |
|-------|------|------------|---------|
| Awaiting Valentina's confirmation | She hasn't confirmed yet | Confirm button visible and enabled | Confirm |
| Awaiting other party | She confirmed, other side hasn't | "Waiting on [name]" status, no button | Wait / navigate away and return |
| Completed | Both sides have confirmed, reached either just now or by reopening from 2.1 | "Trade completed" message, both parties' confirmation shown as done | Back to 2.1 |

---

## Technical Notes

- Per CAP-25/26, the Completed state is only ever reached by both sides independently confirming — this page never lets Valentina force completion on the other party's behalf, mirroring the buyer/seller mutual-confirmation pattern already established for Orders (AD-2) on a conceptual level, though this is a distinct mechanism (trade offers have no payment or money custody involved).
- Both sides confirming at effectively the same moment is not a race that needs special handling: each side's confirmation is an independent, idempotent write to its own field, so simultaneous submission just means both fields land within moments of each other and the trade transitions to Completed as soon as the second one lands — there is no shared mutable state being contended over.
- This page is reachable a second time after the trade is already Completed, via 2.1's list (Completed offers remain visible there, per 2.1's Technical Notes). In that case it renders directly into the Completed state — it never asks a returning visitor to confirm again.

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Set price and condition and choose whether the listing is open to trade (FR-S1, FR-S2).

**Success Criteria:** Listing saved and published with a valid price, condition, and trade preference.

**Entry Points:**
- 1.1 (first time), or directly from My Sales tab / account menu on return visits.
- Also reached by Andrés via the shared `components.create-listing-form` mechanism, `sellerType=business` — from 3.2 the moment his business application exists (Pending or Approved), or as a convenience shortcut from 3.3 once Approved (see Page States).

**Exit Points:**
- → 1.3 Listing Live Confirmation (individual-seller path).
- → 3.2, showing its Listing Published Banner (business path, application still Pending — out of this page's own scope; see 3.2-application-status.md), or → 3.3, showing its own Listing Published Banner (business path, application Approved — out of this page's own scope; see 3.3-commission-balance-management.md). Which of the two this page returns to depends only on the business's current application status at the moment of publish, not on which page the create-listing flow was entered from.

## Technical Notes

- This is the single shared component behind both FR-S1 (Valentina) and FR-S9 (Andrés) — the eligibility check (profile-completion for Valentina; business-application existing, Pending or Approved, for Andrés) happens *before* this page is reachable, not on this page itself. This page never renders an eligibility error; a seller who isn't eligible never reaches it.
- Commission balance never gates reachability or submission of this page for a business submitter — FR-S9 is explicit that creation is never balance-gated, only purchasability is (FR-S6). A Pending or zero-balance business publishes successfully every time; the resulting listing is simply non-purchasable and unbadged until Approved/Funded.
- The Open to Trade toggle's absence for `sellerType=business` is enforced server-side as well (a `openToTrade=true` submission from a business account is rejected as a `DomainError`, per AD-4) — the client-side absence is a UX convenience, not the sole enforcement point.

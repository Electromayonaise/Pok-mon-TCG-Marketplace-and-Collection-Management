## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Upload proof of the peer-to-peer payment (photo or PDF) and confirm "I paid" against the Order (FR-5, CAP-20) — this scenario's climax.

**User Situation:** Returns to TEZG from her banking app after completing the external transfer/QR payment referenced on 3.1.

**Success Criteria:** The Order shows a buyer-paid state only after a comprobante is uploaded *and* she explicitly confirms payment — neither alone is sufficient (FR-5's testable Consequence). Continues into Scenario 04.

**Entry Points:**
- ← 3.1 Purchase Confirmation Screen (after completing the external bank transfer).
- ← 4.1 Orders List, via "Finish payment" on an unpaid/abandoned order (resume path).

**Exit Points:**
- → 4.1 Orders List (on successful "I Paid" confirmation — she now waits for the business's separate confirmation).

## Page States

The upload dropzone's state machine has five primary states plus the Confirm button's own nested sub-states. All are required — this page does not ship happy-path-only.

| State | When | Appearance | Actions |
|-------|------|------------|---------|
| **Idle** | Page loads, or after "Change file" from Success | Dropzone shows upload prompt + 5 MB/PDF disclosure; Confirm button disabled | Tap dropzone to open OS picker |
| **OS-picker-open** | Dropzone tapped | Native OS file/photo chooser is shown (outside TEZG's own UI); requires no TEZG-side visual state beyond having triggered it | File selected → **Uploading**. Chooser dismissed/cancelled with no selection → back to **Idle** (no error, no state change) |
| **Uploading** | A file was selected | Dropzone shows a progress indicator over the filename | None (upload proceeds); a client-side validation failure (wrong type or over 5 MB, detected before any network call) skips directly to **Failure** without ever showing a progress indicator; a server-side failure (network drop, timeout, backend rejection) is reached only after the request was actually sent, and also lands on **Failure** |
| **Success** | Upload completes and is accepted | Dropzone shows the file's thumbnail (image) or filename+icon (PDF), a "Change file" affordance, and a checkmark; Confirm button becomes enabled | Tap "Change file" → back to **Idle** (this is the addition that closes a Phase 3A Reviewer Gate finding: Success is not a dead end — a wrong screenshot can always be replaced before confirming). Tap "Confirm I Paid" → enters the Confirm sub-states below |
| **Failure** | Client or server validation rejects the file, or the upload is interrupted (including the app losing focus/network mid-upload — returning to the app after a dropped connection always resolves to Failure, never a silent retry) | Dropzone shows cause-differentiated copy: "That file's too big — max 5 MB," "We can only accept photos or PDFs," or "Upload didn't go through — check your connection and try again" | Tap the dropzone again to retry — no attempt limit, no cooldown; returns to **OS-picker-open** |

### Confirm I Paid — sub-states (nested under Success)

| Sub-state | When | Appearance | Actions |
|-----------|------|------------|---------|
| Confirming | "Confirm I Paid" tapped | Button disabled, uploaded file remains visible and untouched (own double-submission guard, independent of the upload dropzone's) | None |
| Confirm failed | Confirmation request fails (network/server) | Inline error near the button: "Couldn't confirm your payment — check your connection and try again"; the already-uploaded comprobante is preserved exactly as-is — she does not need to re-upload to retry confirming | Tap "Confirm I Paid" again |
| Confirmed | Confirmation succeeds | Brief transition, navigates to 4.1 Orders List | — |

## Technical Notes

- **Resolves a Phase 3A Reviewer Gate finding on the Platform-field/camera contradiction:** this page has never depended on a native camera API. "Mobile (browser + camera)" in earlier drafts described the user-facing *outcome* (she'll typically photograph a screen or receipt) via the OS's own file/photo chooser, which may itself offer a camera option — TEZG never integrates camera hardware directly. This page's Platform field above states that plainly instead of repeating the ambiguous phrasing.
- Comprobante upload targets under 10 seconds on a typical mobile connection, for a file up to 5 MB (FR-5 Feature-specific NFR).
- No attempt limit or cooldown on Failure retries — SM-C1 (don't let upload-speed pressure degrade comprobante legibility) argues against adding friction here.

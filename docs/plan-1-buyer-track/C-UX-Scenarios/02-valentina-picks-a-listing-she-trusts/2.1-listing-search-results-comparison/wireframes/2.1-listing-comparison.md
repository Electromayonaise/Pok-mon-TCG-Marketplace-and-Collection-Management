# Wireframe: 2.1 Listing Search Results & Comparison

**Purpose:** Make the individual-vs-business CTA contrast — this scenario's climax — vivid enough that it cannot be missed at a glance. Promoted to a dedicated wireframe per the governing plan's explicit call-out; all other pages in this track are judged spine-only sufficient (see the design log's mock-coverage note).

---

**Contrast rules (non-negotiable per DESIGN.md's `cta-business`/`cta-individual` variants):**
- Business CTA: solid fill, `colors.accent`, highest visual weight on the card — "this completes a purchase."
- Individual CTA: outline/ghost style, lower visual weight — "this starts a conversation, not a transaction."
- The seller-type badge is the second-strongest signal (top-left chip on every card), so a user scanning fast catches the distinction even before reading the CTA label.
